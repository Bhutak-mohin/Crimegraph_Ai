How CrimeGraph AI Works

A modular intelligence pipeline built on top of existing CCTNS infrastructure.

1

Data Ingestion

Aggregates FIRs, CCTNS, CDRs, financial records, and OSINT into a unified knowledge graph via Kafka pipelines.

2

Entity Resolution

NLP models extract and disambiguate persons, aliases, locations, vehicles, and organisations from unstructured text.

3

Graph Analysis

Community detection, centrality algorithms, and link prediction reveal hidden connections and gang hierarchies.

4

Analyst Validation

Mandatory human-in-the-loop before any lead is escalated — every prediction is a lead, not an attribution.
